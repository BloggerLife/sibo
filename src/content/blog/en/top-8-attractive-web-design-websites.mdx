## A journey worth sharing

Have you ever imagined working at sea, meeting people from different backgrounds, and building a career in an international environment? For many people, a cruise ship job represents adventure, opportunity, and personal growth. It can also bring important questions: where do you begin, what roles are available, and how should you prepare?

I am **Sibongile Sibanda**, a Zimbabwean cruise ship professional. My own journey has been about more than working at sea: it has also meant learning, growing, and discovering what it takes to pursue an international career. I created Cruise Ship Journey with Sibongile to share useful information and help others approach their own journey with greater understanding.

## From experience to practical support

I know that a first application can feel overwhelming. Candidates may be unsure which roles suit their background, how to present their experience, or what to expect from recruitment. Through this platform, I share industry guidance and practical support, including CV preparation, cover letter writing, and application assistance.

Every person’s path is different, and there is no single formula that guarantees success. Preparation, patience, and a willingness to learn all matter. My aim is to make information easier to understand and encourage candidates to make informed decisions.

If a career at sea interests you, begin by learning about the industry and the roles that match your skills. We guide. We prepare. We empower. Your journey at sea starts with a step.
