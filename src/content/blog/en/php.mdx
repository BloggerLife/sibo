## Your CV is your introduction

For many cruise ship applications, your CV is one of the first ways an employer learns about you. A clear, professional document helps them see how your experience and skills relate to the role.

## Focus on what the job requires

Start with the position you want and read its requirements. Organize your CV so your relevant work experience, qualifications, and practical skills are easy to find. Describe your responsibilities and achievements with concise, specific language. Whenever possible, show how you served customers, worked with a team, followed procedures, solved problems, or stayed organized in a fast-paced setting.

Skills such as communication, teamwork, adaptability, and attention to detail can be useful across cruise ship departments, but connect them to real examples from your own experience. Keep dates and contact details consistent, check spelling, and make sure every claim is accurate. A polished CV should represent you honestly—not promise experience or qualifications you do not have.

## Make each application personal

Avoid sending the exact same CV for every vacancy. Adjust the emphasis to fit the specific position, and use a brief cover letter to explain why you are applying and what relevant experience you bring.
