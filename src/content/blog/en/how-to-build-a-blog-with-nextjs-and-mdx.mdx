## Prepare before you apply

Cruise ship recruitment can feel unfamiliar, especially for a first-time applicant. Requirements and processes differ by employer and position, so begin by choosing a role that matches your experience and reading the official application instructions.

## Present your experience clearly

Your CV should make it easy for a recruiter to understand your work history, qualifications, relevant skills, and achievements. Tailor it to the role rather than sending the same generic document everywhere. Highlight examples of customer service, teamwork, communication, attention to detail, or adapting to a busy workplace. Keep every detail accurate and use a professional, consistent format.

A focused cover letter can explain why your experience fits that particular position. Keep it clear, specific, and truthful.

## Follow a careful process

Some cruise lines recruit directly; others work with recruitment agencies. Confirm that an opportunity and recruiter are legitimate, check the employer’s requirements, and follow the stated application steps. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed employment in exchange for money. No CV or service can guarantee a job.

If you are invited to interview, research the employer and position. Practice describing your experience and how you handle challenges, work with a team, and support guests. Preparation helps you communicate with confidence without exaggerating your qualifications.
