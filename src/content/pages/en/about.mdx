## Welcome to Cruise Ship Journey with Sibongile

Cruise Ship Journey with Sibongile is a cruise ship career guidance and recruitment support platform dedicated to helping individuals pursue employment opportunities in the international cruise ship industry.

Founded by **Sibongile Sibanda**, a Zimbabwean cruise ship professional, our platform was created to help aspiring cruise ship workers understand the industry, prepare for applications, and navigate the journey towards building a career at sea.

We understand that getting a cruise ship job can feel overwhelming, especially for first-time applicants who may not know where to begin. That is why we aim to make the process easier by providing professional CV preparation, cover letter writing, application assistance, industry guidance, and practical support.

## How We Support Your Journey

Our goal is to help candidates present themselves professionally, understand recruitment requirements, and prepare for the different stages of the application process.

We share relevant job opportunities and application information while helping candidates understand what it takes to pursue a career on board an international cruise ship.

At Cruise Ship Journey with Sibongile, we believe that the right information, preparation, and guidance can make a difference in your career journey.

## Our Mission

To empower aspiring cruise ship workers with the information, preparation, and guidance they need to pursue international cruise ship employment opportunities.

## Our Vision

To become a trusted cruise ship career guidance platform connecting aspiring candidates with information, resources, and legitimate employment opportunities across Africa and beyond.

## Our Values

- **Integrity:** Providing honest information and transparent guidance.
- **Professionalism:** Helping candidates prepare professional application documents.
- **Empowerment:** Giving aspiring cruise ship workers the knowledge to take the next step.
- **Transparency:** Being clear about our services, recruitment processes, and limitations.
- **Opportunity:** Supporting candidates from different backgrounds as they pursue their career goals.

> **We guide. We prepare. We empower.**
